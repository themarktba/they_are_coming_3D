HOW TO SURVIVE

THE GOAL

Zombies attack the orphanage every day. Hold them off wave after wave. If you die, or they break into the orphanage, the run is over.

EACH DAY

Build phase: spend your money on guns, melee weapons, armour, barricades, traps and guard towers, then place your defenses.
Night falls: THEY ARE COMING. Survive every wave to get paid.

ZOMBIES

Walkers, Runners, Helmet zombies (shoot the helmet off first), Revivers (they get back up unless you finish the head), Crawlers, Bloaters (they explode), Brutes. Every 5th day something much bigger shows up.

CONTROLS (rebind in Settings)

BUILD MODE

Click an item to buy. Structures follow your cursor: click to place, R to rotate, right-click to cancel, hold SHIFT to place several. Click a placed structure to sell it. WASD pans the map, the wheel zooms.

LOADOUT & HEALTH

You carry 4 weapons into a fight; click an owned weapon in the shop to equip or stash it, and upgrade your favourites. Health does not regenerate: heal in the shop, eat bananas, use medkits, or hire a medic.

DEFENSES

You walk straight through your own builds, but your bullets don't: stand on a platform to shoot over your barricades.

SETTINGS

KEYBINDS

Click an action, then press the new key. ESC cancels.
